About JetLink

A small, fast link shortener built for people who share things for a living.

Our mission

Long links are ugly, break in messages and tell you nothing after you share them. JetLink exists to fix that: turn any link into a short, clean one you can share anywhere — and learn from every click it gets.

What we do

We shorten links on multiple domains, generate QR codes, build social preview cards and media links, and count every click with privacy-first analytics. Creators use us for video drops, marketers for campaigns, businesses for print, and developers through our API.

Why is it free?

Shortening a link costs very little to run. We would rather grow a big, happy free user base than charge people for the basics. If paid extras ever arrive, the free plan described on the pricing page stays free.

Privacy, briefly

We count clicks, not people. Visitor data is aggregated (country, device type, referrer) and we never sell it. The full details live in our privacy policy.
